# Article Catalog — kayu.co.id

## Scope

This catalog expands the 18 parent topics in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md` into 108 mutually distinct briefs. It plans a complete wood-material knowledge system; it does not authorize mass publication. Every page must pass its evidence gate and same-domain overlap review before publication.

Domain boundaries:

- `/artikel/` owns neutral knowledge. Existing product and service routes own actual offers, availability, quotes, delivery, warranty, and company-specific claims.
- Structural content is education and briefing only; a qualified structural engineer using current authorized standards owns project design and acceptance.
- Furniture style, ergonomics, room planning, and finished-furniture buying belong to `furnitur.co.id`; this domain owns wood-material behavior and specification.
- Fire, chemical, health, pesticide, and machine-safety content requires applicable primary evidence and qualified review. No article is a substitute for an engineer, K3 professional, industrial hygienist, chemist, licensed pest professional, or regulator.
- Cannibalization is tested only within `kayu.co.id`. Other domains may independently cover the same topic.

Catalog statistics: **18 topics; 108 briefs; 12 P0, 36 P1, 42 P2, and 18 P3**.

Priority legend:

- **P0 / W1:** bounded foundation cluster to publish and measure first.
- **P1 / W2:** high-value lifecycle, selection, and failure-prevention coverage after W1 passes review.
- **P2 / W3:** professional depth, fabrication, operations, and advanced decision support.
- **P3 / W4:** history, specialist, or evidence-intensive long-tail coverage; publish only with strong source assets.
