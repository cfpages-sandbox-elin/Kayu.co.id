## Purpose

This file is the reusable pre-writing evidence foundation for the complete `kayu.co.id` catalog. It grounds wood anatomy and variability, Indonesian species, moisture and processing, solid wood and panels, durability, structural/fire/chemical boundaries, legality, procurement, workshop safety, and lifecycle decisions.

It is not an article, licensed standard, structural design, chemical treatment instruction, legal opinion, or proof of any offered product. Article writers must cite the original sources below, not this file.

## Evidence grades

- **A-ID:** current Indonesian law, official Indonesian regulator service, or official BSN record.
- **B-STD:** official standards-body record; the public page proves identity, status, and visible scope only.
- **C-PRI:** government or recognized primary technical/research publication.
- **D-GUIDE:** recognized international safety or industry guidance whose jurisdiction and application limits must remain visible.
- **E-GATE:** project, product, licensed-text, test, site, or professional evidence still required.

## How to use this file

1. Start from the matching `KAY-*` coverage row.
2. Use only facts within the cited source's visible scope and jurisdiction.
3. Separate species averages, clear-specimen tests, commercial grade, processed product, and installed-system performance.
4. Resolve every mapped gate before using exact values, compliance language, safety instructions, or product claims.
5. Recheck laws, standards, certificates, labels, reports, and links when outlines begin.

## Cross-catalog fact bank

1. Wood species, an individual piece, a graded lot, a processed panel, and an installed system are different evidence objects.
2. Moisture, test method, specimen orientation/condition, grade, and service exposure must travel with property values.
3. A standards catalogue proves identity/status/visible scope, not licensed requirements or product compliance.
4. A foreign method or rule is not Indonesian law; a certificate or chain-of-custody system does not by itself prove every product characteristic.
5. Commercial availability, price, capacity, warranty, legality, and delivered conformity require current project and transaction evidence.
